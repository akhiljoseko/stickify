## Brand & Style
The brand personality is authoritative, reliable, and hyper-efficient, catering to logistics managers and warehouse operators who prioritize speed and accuracy. The design style follows a **Corporate / Modern** aesthetic with a strong emphasis on utility and clarity. 

The interface minimizes visual noise to ensure that label data and print statuses are the primary focus. High productivity is achieved through a structured hierarchy, ample white space to prevent cognitive overload, and a layout that feels stable and engineered. The emotional response should be one of "controlled confidence"—a tool that works as hard as the hardware it controls.

## Colors
The palette is anchored by **Industrial Blue** (#004B87), a deep, professional tone that evokes trust and technical stability. This color is reserved for primary actions and brand-heavy elements. 

The workspace uses a foundation of **Clean Whites** and **Soft Grays** to provide high contrast for text and barcode elements.
- **Primary:** Industrial Blue for primary buttons, active states, and navigation headers.
- **Secondary:** Light Steel Blue for subtle backgrounds, secondary buttons, and row hovering.
- **Success (Tertiary):** "Completed" status and successful print notifications.
- **Neutral:** Slate grays for secondary text, borders, and metadata.
- **Status Tones:** Warning (Amber #F59E0B) for low ink/media and Danger (Rose #E11D48) for print errors or paper jams.

### Dark Theme Colors
The dark theme corresponds to the light theme but provides a comfortable, high-contrast palette for low-light environments (e.g., night shifts in warehouses):
- **Primary:** Dark Industrial Blue (#a3c9ff) as primary accent color for active elements.
- **Background & Surface:** Deep Slate Blue (#0d1c2e) for overall canvas and card containers to reduce glare.
- **On-Surface:** Pale Slate Blue (#eaf1ff) for primary text and labels to ensure high readability.
- **Secondary:** Dark Steel Gray (#c0c7cc) for subtle background, borders, and secondary controls.
- **Success (Tertiary):** Emerald Green (#4edea3) for positive statuses and print completion indicator.
- **Error:** Light Red (#ffb4ab) for highly visible alerts and printer error statuses.

## Typography
Typography is optimized for rapid scanning and data density. **Hanken Grotesk** is used for headlines to provide a modern, sharp architectural feel. **Inter** is the workhorse for all body copy and UI controls due to its exceptional legibility at small sizes and high x-height.

For technical data—such as SKU numbers, barcode strings, and printer IP addresses—**JetBrains Mono** is utilized to ensure individual characters are indistinguishable and aligned. On mobile, headlines scale down significantly (Display LG becomes 24px) to maintain space for tabular data.

## Layout & Spacing
The design system employs a **Fixed Grid** philosophy for the main workspace to ensure that label preview areas remain consistent in size, regardless of screen width. 

- **Desktop:** A 12-column grid with a max-width of 1440px. Sidebars for printer settings and asset libraries are fixed at 280px.
- **Tablet:** 8-column grid. Sidebars collapse into drawers to maximize the canvas.
- **Mobile:** 4-column grid. Focus shifts to status monitoring and simple "Start/Stop" print controls.

Spacing follows a strict 8px base unit. Data-heavy lists use a compact 4px vertical padding to maximize the number of visible rows, while marketing or onboarding pages use 32px+ gaps to feel more breathable.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is communicated through **Tonal Layers** rather than heavy shadows to maintain a "flat and fast" feel. 

- **Level 0 (Background):** Soft gray (#F8FAFC) for the application canvas.
- **Level 1 (Surface):** Pure white cards for the primary workspace and data tables. Use a 1px solid border (#E2E8F0) instead of a shadow.
- **Level 2 (Popovers):** Modals and dropdown menus use a subtle, extra-diffused ambient shadow (0px 4px 20px rgba(0, 75, 135, 0.08)) to distinguish them from the base layer.
- **Active State:** Selected items use a 2px Industrial Blue interior border to indicate focus without shifting layout positions.

## Shapes
This design system uses a **Soft (1)** roundedness profile. A 0.25rem (4px) corner radius is applied to buttons, input fields, and checkboxes. This subtle rounding maintains a professional, "industrial" appearance while feeling modern and accessible. Large containers like the label preview area may use 8px (0.5rem) to soften the overall composition, while status indicators remain at 4px to maintain their punchy, alert-like quality.

## Components

### Buttons & Controls
- **Primary Button:** Solid Industrial Blue with white text. High-contrast and clear.
- **Secondary Button:** Light Steel Blue background with Industrial Blue text. Used for "Add New" or "Export" actions.
- **Print Action:** A specialized large-format button with a "Print" icon, using a slightly bolder weight to signify the primary goal of the software.

### Status Indicators (Pills)
- **Printing:** Pulsing blue border with a subtle blue tint background. Includes a "spinning" sync icon.
- **Completed:** Solid emerald background with white checkmark.
- **Draft/Idle:** Light gray background with slate text.
- **Error:** Bold red background with white exclamation icon.

### Input Fields
- Labels are always top-aligned using `body-sm`. 
- Fields use a 1px slate border that thickens and turns Industrial Blue on focus.
- Monospaced font is used for inputs requiring precise SKU or serial data.

### Data Tables
- Header cells use `label-mono` with a light gray background.
- Row heights are tight (40px) for density.
- Zebra-striping is applied using the `secondary_color` on even rows to aid horizontal scanning.

### Label Preview Card
- A dedicated container with a subtle inner shadow to simulate the physical label. 
- Features a "Scale to Fit" toggle and a high-contrast border to separate the label "stock" from the UI background.
